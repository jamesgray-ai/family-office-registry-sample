# Capital Call Processing SOP

**Owner:** Finance (Controller). **Runs:** when a capital call notice arrives in the capital calls inbox.

## Steps

1. Save the notice to the capital calls inbox folder. The capital-call-preparer agent picks it up.
2. The agent reads the notice, checks it against the commitment schedule and today's cash, and drafts the payment request, or writes a flag.
3. **Controller review.** Open the draft payment request. Confirm the fund, entity, amount and due date against the notice.
4. **Instructions changed?** If the agent flagged changed wire instructions, call the fund administrator at the number on file (never a number in the notice) before anything else. Record the call in the payment file.
5. **Approve.** The Controller approves the payment in the bank portal. A second approver signs off on any call over $500,000.
6. **File.** Move the notice, the record and the approval to the fund's folder.

## If something goes wrong

- Notice for an unknown entity: send to General Counsel.
- Agent did not pick up a notice within one business day: process it by hand with these steps and tell the operations team.
