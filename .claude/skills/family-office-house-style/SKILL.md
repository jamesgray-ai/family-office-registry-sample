# Family Office House Style

A reference skill: the standards every family-facing document meets. Other skills and agents load it when they draft something the family will read.

## Voice

- Plain English. Write for a family member who is smart and busy, not for an analyst.
- Lead with what changed and what needs a decision, then the detail.
- Explain an investment term the first time it appears in a document, in a few plain words: "TVPI (total value to paid-in: what each dollar invested is worth today)". After that, the short form is fine.
- Name people by role ("the Controller"), never by first name, in anything that may be shared outside the office.

## Numbers

- Currency in US dollars with no decimals in summaries ($2.4M, $350K); full figures in tables.
- Percentages to one decimal place.
- Every figure carries its source and "as of" date in a footnote.

## Format

- One-page summary first; detail in appendices.
- Headings are statements ("Private equity returned 4.1% this quarter"), not labels ("Private equity").
- Decisions needed are listed together at the top, each with its owner and due date.

## Confidentiality

- Mark every document "Confidential: prepared for the family".
- Never include account numbers, tax IDs or personal addresses; refer to accounts by their nickname in the account register.
